year: '2013'

intro: HP tasked us with developing a platform that could help demonstrate large pieces of hardware without the need for having the actual product. Using the Unity 3D gaming platform for an explicitly B2B marketing piece, we were able to allow the manipulation photo-real models in a 3D space. As well as publish to multiple tablet devices simultaneously. The UI allows for zoom in/out, X/Y/Z rotation, moving from one component or feature to another by use of the right side slider, bottom carousel or directly on the model itself.
