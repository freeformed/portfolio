Who me?

Info

Inspiration surrounds us. It fuels our need to create and transform fleeting ideas into tangible expressions.

When not pushing pixels, I enjoy time at the beach, fishing, family adventures, obscure boardgames, and exploring new eateries. I brew a mean kölsch, and usually have a barolo fermenting in a closet. My dog is weirdly short, missing a few teeth, and I wouldn't have her any other way.
